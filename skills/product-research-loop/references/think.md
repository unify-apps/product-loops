# Think: from the sheet to a shape

Think the product through for the persona, before anyone builds. Write `thinking.md` in the out folder with
exactly these `##` sections, in this order. Keep each one short: a few lines or a small table.

## Sections

1. `## Persona`
   - The person who uses it every day: their role, where they work, how big the place is.
   - What they use for this job today (paper, a spreadsheet, a rival). That sets the bar.
   - The words they use for things. Those become the product's labels.
   - What goes wrong for them if the product is wrong.
   - One line for each other person who touches it (the buyer, a customer, a helper).

2. `## The job`
   - The persona's job as numbered steps, from the first moment to the end result. These are the "job steps"
     every module must answer. Example: 1. A patient asks for a visit. 2. Staff find a free slot. ...
   - Say which steps this product covers and which stay in other tools.

3. `## What this really is`
   - One line: "Not X. It is Y." Name the real thing, not the obvious category.
   - The one thing underneath, in one sentence (for example "a booking is a time slot plus a person plus a reason").

4. `## Principle`
   - One bold rule that every later choice is checked against. It must be clear enough to say no to things.

5. `## Flow`
   - Walk the main path as the persona, as numbered steps: where they start, what they decide at each step, and
     what the product has already done for them. Add the states that matter: first use, empty, an error, a
     no-show or a change of plan.

6. `## Modules`
   - The candidate modules, as a table. Every module serves a job step. A module that serves no step is not
     a row in this table: write it under **Cut** in the next section, with the reason.

     | Module | Job step it serves | Matrix rows | Why the persona needs it |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | Online booking | 1, 2 | C01, C04 | Staff stop answering the phone for every booking |

7. `## The simplicity cut`
   - Three short lists: **Keep** (first release), **Later** (with the reason, "for now"), **Cut** (with the
     reason). A field, screen or module stays only if it changes a decision the persona makes.
   - Depth before breadth: a few complete modules beat many thin ones.

Add `## Ideas` at the end only for ideas no page proves yet. They never go in the ranked list.

## Rules

- Use the sheet and the rival files as the facts. Do not invent numbers.
- Where there is a choice, pick one and say why in one line, with the other option in a few words.
- Write plainly. A sentence that could be about any product is cut.
