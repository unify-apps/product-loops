# FreshBooks

- Home page: https://www.freshbooks.com/en-gb/
- Who it is for: Freelancers, solopreneurs and small businesses with employees or contractors, including creatives, agencies, trades, consulting and legal (home page).
- Price: Lite £16/mo (5 clients), Plus £30/mo (50 clients), Premium £42/mo (unlimited), Select custom; 30-day trial; Advanced Payments add-on £15/mo; team members £8/user/mo.
