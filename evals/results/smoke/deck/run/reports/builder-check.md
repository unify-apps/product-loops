# Builder check: briefs/builder-check.md

Read as the person who must build the deck alone. At most 3 questions whose answer changes what gets built, each with the default I would take.

## 1. What exactly is the ask on the last slide?
The goal is "agrees to a small pilot in a few clinics", but facts.md gives no clinic count, length, start date or pilot price. Only the list price (£120 per clinic per month) is known. The ask slide's wording and numbers depend on this.
- **Default:** ask for "a pilot in a few of your clinics", quote the list price of £120 per clinic per month, and invent no clinic count, length, discount or start date. Mark the pilot shape as "to agree together".

## 2. Is the deck read alone or presented live?
G3 says "the reader alone (the COO)", but the ask says it is for "a first meeting". A deck read alone needs full sentences and self-explaining slides; a presented deck needs short headlines with the talk carried by the speaker. This changes the text on every slide.
- **Default:** take G3 as written: built to be read alone, so each slide has a full-sentence headline and 2 to 4 short lines that make sense without a speaker.

## 3. Do we show a cost of no-shows, or only percentages?
The coverage scan notes there are no appointment volumes or value per appointment, so the cost cannot be sized. The angle says no-shows are "a cost she already named". Whether a money slide exists, and how strong the claim is, depends on this. The 8% to 5% pilot result is also marked as an estimate.
- **Default:** no money figure and no slide sizing the cost. Show percentages only (her about 9%; our pilot 8% to 5% in 3 months, labelled as an estimate from 3 clinics) and say the cost can be sized together with her appointment numbers in the pilot.
